5. Bookings and payments

  • Price. Musicians set an hourly rate. A booking costs the hourly rate times its length, unless both people agree on a different total in chat. The booker also pays a service fee shown before checkout, which covers card processing.
  • Request and hold. Requesting a booking places a hold on the booker's card. The musician has 48 hours to accept. The card is charged only if the musician accepts. If they decline or don't answer, the hold is released.
  • Getting paid. Setlist keeps a commission of 8% of the booking price from the musician's side. The rest is released to the musician 48 hours after the gig, unless the booker reports a problem. Payouts are made through our payment processor, Stripe, and musicians must complete Stripe's identity checks to receive money.
  • Cancellations. A booker who cancels at least 72 hours before the gig gets a full refund. Cancellations closer to the gig may not be refunded. If a musician cancels, the booker gets a full refund.
  • Problems. If something goes wrong, the booker can report a problem from the booking within 48 hours after the gig. Payment is paused while it's sorted out. The musician can refund or respond, and Setlist may decide the outcome.
  • Keep it in the app. Don't ask to be paid outside Setlist for someone you met on Setlist. Payments made outside the app aren't protected and may lead to your account being closed.

Payments are processed by Stripe and are also subject to Stripe's terms.
