SETLIST

Privacy Policy

Last updated November 1, 2026

DRAFT · to be reviewed by a lawyer before launch

The short version: We collect what's needed to run Setlist: your account, your profile, your approximate city, your messages, and booking and payment records. We don't sell your information or show you ads, and you can delete your account in the app at any time.

What we collect

  • Account: your email address and password (stored securely, never visible to us), or your Apple ID sign-in if you use Sign in with Apple.
  • Profile: your name or organization's name, account type, instruments, styles, bio, hourly rate, availability, profile photos, and performance videos.
  • Location: if you allow it, your phone's location while you use the app, to find your city. We save only an approximate location (rounded to about 1 km) and your city name. Your exact location is never stored or shown to others.
  • Messages and activity: your chats, rate offers, gig posts, applications, "Looking for" requests, reviews, saved musicians, blocks, and reports.
  • Bookings and payments: booking details, amounts, and payment status. Card numbers are handled by Stripe; we never see or store them. To receive payouts or get a verified badge, Stripe checks your identity; we only receive the result, not your ID documents.
  • Notifications: if you allow alerts, a code from Apple that lets us send them to your phone.
  • Crash reports: when the app crashes, a technical report (app version, iOS version, phone model, and where in the code it crashed). It doesn't include your messages or profile.

How we use it

  • To run Setlist: show your profile, match you with gigs and musicians near you, deliver messages and alerts, and process bookings and payouts.
  • To keep people safe: messages, names, bios, and posts are checked automatically for offensive words, and blocked attempts are logged. Videos get an automatic safety check. The Setlist team reviews reports and may read reported messages.
  • To fix problems and improve the app, for example using crash reports.
  • To follow the law: taxes, refunds, disputes, and copyright notices.

Who can see what

  • Other users see your profile (name, photos, videos, bio, instruments, rate, city, availability, reviews). Only the people in a chat can read it. If you block someone, they can't see your profile or message you. You can hide yourself from search in Settings.
  • Service providers that run parts of Setlist for us, only to provide their service: Supabase (database, sign-in, and file storage), Mux (video hosting and automatic video safety checks), Stripe (payments, payouts, and identity checks), Apple (Sign in with Apple and notifications), and our email provider (account emails).
  • When the law requires it, or to protect someone's safety, or as part of a sale of the business.

We don't sell your personal information, and we don't use it for advertising or share it with advertisers.

How long we keep it

We keep your information while you have an account. When you delete your account (Profile → Account → Delete account), we remove your profile details, photos, and videos, stop your alerts, and remove you from search. Records of past bookings and payments are kept, shown as "Deleted user," because we're required to keep them for refunds, taxes, and disputes. Messages you sent in a chat stay visible to the other person, under "Deleted user."

Your choices

  • Edit your profile anytime in the app.
  • Turn location or notifications off in your iPhone's Settings.
  • Delete your account in the app.
  • Ask us for a copy of your information, or to correct or delete it, by emailing us. Depending on where you live, you may have more rights under local law, and we'll honor them.

Security

Information is sent over encrypted connections and protected by access rules so people can only see what they're allowed to. No system is perfect, so please use a strong password and keep it private.

Children

Setlist is for people 18 and older. We don't knowingly collect information from children. If you think a child has an account, contact us and we'll remove it.

Changes

If we change this policy in an important way, we'll tell you in the app.
