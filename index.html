Copyright notice

If you own the rights to a work and believe a performance video on Setlist infringes them, submit a notice under the Digital Millennium Copyright Act (17 U.S.C. § 512). The video is hidden as soon as we can identify it, and the musician is told and can respond.

Counter-notices

Musicians whose video was removed can file a counter-notice from the Setlist app (Profile → Video). Under the law, the video is restored after 10 business days unless the person who sent the notice tells us they've filed a lawsuit.

Repeat infringers

Accounts with 3 valid takedowns can no longer post videos.
